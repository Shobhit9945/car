# Horizon Car Lounge — Coding School Final Project

## Files
- `index.html` — Home page
- `contact.html` — Contact and enquiry form
- `a-propos.html` — About the agency
- `equipe.html` — Team profiles
- `avis.html` — Customer reviews and review form
- `vehicule-audis.html` — Audi A8L
- `vehicule-porsche.html` — Porsche 911 Carrera GTS
- `vehicule-mercedes.html` — Mercedes-Benz S-Class
- `vehicule-audi.html` — Audi RS6 Avant
- `vehicule-tesla.html` — Tesla Model S Plaid
- `style.css` — One shared stylesheet
- `script.js` — Mobile navigation and demo form interactions

## How to use on Coding School
1. Create an HTML/CSS project named exactly `Final Project - 2026/2027`.
2. Upload all ten HTML files, `style.css` and `script.js` into the project root.
3. Open `index.html` to preview the website.

## Important before submission
- The contact details, address, testimonials, vehicle specifications, prices and promotions are sample content because the brief does not provide real company data.
- The contact and review forms are front-end demonstrations only. They show a confirmation message but do not transmit or store submissions.
- Replace the map placeholder with the real showroom location.
- Add licensed audio (`assets/rs6-engine-sound.mp3`) and a licensed Tesla demonstration video if required. The relevant player areas are included as placeholders.
- Vehicle images are loaded from Unsplash URLs and therefore require an internet connection.
- Verify all vehicle specifications and legal rental conditions before presenting the site as a real business website.
